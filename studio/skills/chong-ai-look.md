# Chống "AI-look"
Make the result look designed by a person, not generated: clean, purposeful, printable.

- One clear focal point; everything else supports it. Remove clutter and filler objects.
- Visual hierarchy: one dominant element, one secondary, small details last.
- Leave real whitespace (or transparent space) around the main shapes; do not fill every gap.
- Balanced, intentional layout: align elements to a grid or a clear axis.
- Limited palette: at most 4-6 colours, taken from the current palette unless the goal says otherwise.
- Typography with a purpose: at most two typefaces, readable at print size, no warped or melted letters.
- No glow, lens flare, bokeh, chrome, plastic shine, heavy gradients, drop shadows or "AI sparkle".
- No extra fingers, broken symmetry, mushy details, random text-like scribbles or fake signatures.
- Flat, crisp edges suitable for screen/DTG printing; avoid soft semi-transparent halos.

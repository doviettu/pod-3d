# File in áo POD
Rules for a print-ready garment design file.

- Transparent background (PNG with real alpha), unless the brief gives a background colour.
- Hard edges: every pixel either fully opaque or fully transparent; no faint halo around shapes.
- Lines at least 0.5 mm and dots at least 1 mm at print size (about 6 px and 12 px at 300 DPI).
- Keep important elements inside the print area with a small margin; nothing cut by the canvas edge unless intended.
- Colours that print well: avoid neon RGB and very dark blues on black shirts; keep contrast against the shirt colour.
- No mockup, no fabric texture, no shirt outline, no shadows or lighting baked into the art.
- Text spelled exactly as given; no invented logos or brand names.
